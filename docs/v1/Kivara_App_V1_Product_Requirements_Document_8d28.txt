KIVARA HEALTHCARE
VERSION 1 PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT
Digital Healthcare Staffing Platform • First Draft • July 2026
1. Product Vision
Kivara will provide one connected digital system with three private experiences: a CNA mobile app, a Facility Portal, and a Kivara Master Admin Portal.
The platform will manage the workflow from application and credentialing through confirmed assignments, GPS-verified attendance, timesheets, payroll-ready hours, and facility billing.
2. Version 1 Success Flow
Apply → Get Cleared → Become Work Ready → See Eligible Shifts → Request Shift → Kivara Confirms Assignment → Shift Locks → GPS Clock In/Out → Timesheet → Kivara Approval → Payroll-Ready Data + Facility Invoice
3. User Types
CNA — mobile-first worker app; sees only their own profile, credentials, shifts, time, messages, and pay status.
Facility User — secure portal; sees only authorized facility company and locations.
Kivara Admin — master dashboard; agency-wide access according to assigned role.
4. Non-Negotiable Business Rules
Confirmed Means Scheduled — when Kivara confirms an assignment, the CNA is scheduled to work and is not removed merely because another worker becomes available.
No Casual Facility Cancellation — facilities do not receive a simple Cancel CNA button. A confirmed-assignment exception requires a documented reason and audit record.
One Position Reopens — if one confirmed CNA calls out, only that assignment reopens. Other confirmed CNAs keep their shifts.
Eligibility Before Visibility — CNAs see or request only shifts they are eligible to work.
One Connected Record Chain — staffing request → shift position → assignment → GPS punches → timesheet → payroll-ready record + invoice.
No Silent Edits — sensitive changes preserve the original value, new value, user, time, and reason.
5. CNA App — Version 1 Requirements
Secure account creation, login, password reset, and session management.
Digital application and onboarding checklist.
Personal profile with controlled editing.
Secure document upload and replacement.
Credential statuses: Missing, Uploaded, Pending Review, Approved, Expiring Soon, Expired, Rejected.
Work Ready status and action items.
Eligible available shifts with filters.
Shift details: facility, location, date/time, unit, pay rate, requirements, and instructions.
Request Shift and request-status tracking.
Confirmed schedule with clear language: Confirmed — You Are Scheduled to Work.
GPS/geofence-assisted clock-in and clock-out.
Shift-day states: Confirmed, Ready to Clock In, Clocked In, On Shift, Clocked Out, Timesheet Submitted.
Timesheet review and correction requests.
Messages, alerts, notification preferences, support, forms/resources, and work-area preferences.
Approved-hours and payroll-status visibility; official payroll documents remain with the payroll provider.
6. Facility Portal — Version 1 Requirements
Separate secure workspace for each facility company, with multiple locations when needed.
Authorized facility users with role-based permissions.
Create staffing requests with location, unit, date, time, CNA quantity, requirements, instructions, and contacts.
Track request states: Draft, Submitted, Open, Partially Filled, Fully Staffed, In Progress, Completed, Billed.
View confirmed assignments and limited work-related CNA information approved by Kivara.
View attendance statuses: Scheduled, Arrived, Clocked In, On Shift, Clocked Out.
Review timesheets or submit a documented discrepancy.
View invoices and billing status.
Message Kivara and submit urgent operational issues.
No access to other facilities’ data or unrestricted CNA GPS history.
7. Kivara Master Admin Portal — Version 1 Requirements
Agency command-center dashboard.
CNA application review, onboarding progress, and Work Ready controls.
Credential review, expiration tracking, and facility-specific requirements.
Facility company, location, contact, and permission management.
Staffing request review and shift publishing.
Eligibility checks and shift-request review.
Confirm Assignment action.
Call-out, no-show, and replacement workflow without disturbing unrelated confirmed assignments.
Live attendance status and GPS/time exceptions.
Timesheet review, correction history, approval, and lock controls.
Payroll-ready data creation/export status.
Invoice draft generation from approved billable hours.
Messages, alerts, reports, documents, and audit history.
8. Roles & Permissions
Owner / Super Admin — full system access.
Operations Manager — facilities, requests, assignments, schedule, attendance, call-outs.
Recruiter / Credentialing — applications, onboarding, profiles, credentials, screening statuses.
Payroll / Billing — approved timesheets, payroll-ready hours, invoices, payment status.
Read Only / Auditor — authorized view access without record changes.
Facility Admin — authorized locations, users, staffing, assignments, timesheets, invoices.
DON / Scheduler — request staff, view assignments, communicate with Kivara.
Shift Supervisor — today’s assigned CNAs, attendance, timesheet review, incident reporting.
Billing Contact — invoices, statements, payment history.
9. Core Records
CNA profile and employment record.
Facility company, locations, authorized users, requirements, rates, and billing terms.
Credential record with document, dates, status, verification, reviewer, and history.
Staffing Request with one or more requested positions.
Assignment connecting one CNA to one position.
GPS Punch with time, type, geofence result, exception, and correction history.
Timesheet with scheduled/actual hours, breaks, review, approval, and lock status.
Payroll Record with approved hours, pay rate, period, and export/provider status.
Invoice with shifts, billable hours, rates, dates, and status.
Audit Event recording who did what, when, and why.
10. GPS & Timekeeping Requirements
Use location for defined work-verification purposes, not unrestricted off-duty tracking.
Verify proximity to the assigned facility geofence at clock-in and clock-out.
Record location-verification result and exception status.
Allow controlled manual review when GPS is unavailable or inaccurate.
Do not automatically penalize a CNA solely because of a GPS exception.
Facility users see operational status, not unrestricted location history.
Time corrections require authorization, reason, and audit history.
11. Integrations
Background Screening — invite candidate and receive workflow status; provider handles sensitive screening.
Drug Screening — order when required and show status.
Payroll — send approved hours; provider handles taxes, withholding, direct deposit, pay stubs, and W-2s.
Accounting — sync invoice/payment references; accounting platform handles books and reconciliation.
E-signature — track document version, signer, completion, final copy, and evidence.
Email/SMS/Push — deliver Kivara-created alerts.
Maps/GPS — facility address verification, directions, distance, and geofence services.
12. Security & Privacy Requirements
Unique user accounts; no shared credentials.
Multi-factor authentication for privileged Kivara accounts.
Role-based access and strict facility data isolation.
Encryption in transit and at rest.
Secure file uploads and controlled document access.
Sensitive data minimization and masking.
Banking data remains with payroll provider whenever possible.
Background and drug-screen details restricted to authorized users.
Secure backups, monitoring, patching, account recovery, and incident response.
Separate development/test and production environments.
Kivara controls the domain, cloud accounts, source-code repository, database ownership, app-store accounts, and production credentials.

14. Out of Scope for Version 1
Continuous live-map tracking throughout an entire shift.
AI-based automatic shift matching.
Public worker marketplace.
Built-in payroll tax processing.
Built-in background-check adjudication engine.
Built-in laboratory/drug-testing system.
Instant-pay wallet owned by Kivara.
Advanced forecasting, complex analytics, and automated collections.
15. Build Stages
Stage 1 — Interactive Prototype: clickable CNA, Facility, and Admin journeys using test data only.
Stage 2 — Core Working App: authentication, roles, profiles, facilities, documents, credentials, requests, shifts, assignments, notifications.
Stage 3 — GPS, Timekeeping & Money Flow: geofences, punches, exceptions, timesheets, payroll-ready hours, invoice generation.
Stage 4 — Integrations & Launch Testing: connect selected providers and test complete workflows before real sensitive data.
16. Open Business Decisions Before Development

Overtime rules and approval workflow.
Facility billing terms and rate structure.
Confirmed-shift exception and cancellation-pay policy.
Exact credential requirements.
Background-screening provider and workflow.
Drug-screen policy and provider.
Geofence radius and clock-in/out windows.
Timesheet approval deadlines and dispute process.
Data-retention periods.
Final privacy notice, location disclosure/consent, user terms, and facility contract language.
Selected technology/build path and development budget.
17. MVP Acceptance Standard
Version 1 is ready for controlled pilot testing when a test CNA can complete the full digital flow from application through a confirmed assignment and GPS-verified timesheet; a test facility can request staff, view the confirmed assignment, review the timesheet, and receive an invoice; and a Kivara admin can manage the workflow with role controls and an audit trail without re-entering the same shift data into multiple places.
