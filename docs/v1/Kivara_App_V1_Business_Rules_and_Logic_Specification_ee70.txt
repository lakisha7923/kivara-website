KIVARA HEALTHCARE
VERSION 1 BUSINESS RULES & LOGIC SPECIFICATION
Rules the app must enforce automatically • First Draft • July 2026
Purpose: Define the operational rules, statuses, permissions, automatic checks, exceptions, and record transitions required for the Kivara Version 1 app. Rules already approved are marked APPROVED. Items that require a future business decision are marked OPEN DECISION.
1. CNA Work Ready Logic
APPROVED — A CNA is not Work Ready until required onboarding steps are complete and all required core credentials are approved.
APPROVED — A missing, rejected, or expired required credential can remove Work Ready status or restrict affected shift eligibility.
APPROVED — Facility-specific requirements affect eligibility for that facility without necessarily making the CNA inactive everywhere.
APPROVED — Only an authorized Kivara role may mark or restore Work Ready status.
OPEN DECISION — Final list of core credentials and screening requirements.
2. Shift Visibility & Eligibility
APPROVED — A CNA should see or request only shifts for which the system considers them eligible.
APPROVED — Eligibility checks include active employment/Work Ready status, required credentials, facility-specific requirements, schedule conflicts, and configured work-area rules.
APPROVED — The app should flag overtime risk before confirmation.
OPEN DECISION — Whether overtime risk blocks a request, requires admin approval, or only shows a warning.
OPEN DECISION — Exact travel-distance rules and whether CNAs can override them.
3. Shift Request Logic
APPROVED — A CNA may request an eligible open position.
APPROVED — A request does not guarantee the shift until Kivara confirms the assignment.
APPROVED — Kivara reviews the request and system eligibility results.
APPROVED — One staffing request may contain multiple positions; each CNA assignment is tracked separately.
OPEN DECISION — Whether some shifts may use automatic first-qualified confirmation later. Not included in Version 1 unless specifically approved.
4. Confirmed Means Scheduled
APPROVED — The admin action is Confirm Assignment.
APPROVED — Once confirmed, the CNA sees: CONFIRMED — YOU ARE SCHEDULED TO WORK.
APPROVED — A confirmed CNA is not removed merely because another worker becomes available.
APPROVED — The facility cannot casually cancel or replace a confirmed CNA through a simple button.
APPROVED — Any confirmed-assignment exception requires a reason, authorized action, timestamp, and audit record.
OPEN DECISION — Cancellation-pay or minimum-pay policy for facility-caused confirmed-shift exceptions.
5. Multi-Position Staffing Requests
APPROVED — If a facility requests multiple CNAs, each requested position has its own fill status.
APPROVED — Confirmed assignments remain intact while Kivara fills remaining open positions.
APPROVED — If one confirmed CNA calls out, only that CNA's position reopens.
APPROVED — Other confirmed CNAs keep their assignments.
6. Call-Out, No-Show & Replacement Logic
APPROVED — A call-out creates an urgent admin alert and reopens only the affected position.
APPROVED — Eligible available CNAs may be notified of the replacement opening.
APPROVED — A replacement is not scheduled until Kivara confirms the new assignment.
APPROVED — The facility is updated when the replacement is confirmed.
OPEN DECISION — Call-out notice requirements, attendance points, disciplinary rules, and emergency exceptions.
7. GPS & Clock-In Logic
APPROVED — Clock-in is tied to a confirmed assignment.
APPROVED — The app checks the worker's proximity to the assigned facility geofence at clock-in and clock-out.
APPROVED — A GPS failure or outside-geofence result creates an exception for review; it does not automatically accuse or penalize the CNA.
APPROVED — Facilities see operational status, not unrestricted GPS history.
APPROVED — Location collection is limited to defined work-verification purposes.
OPEN DECISION — Exact geofence radius.
OPEN DECISION — How many minutes before/after scheduled start the Clock In button becomes active.
OPEN DECISION — Break-tracking requirements.
8. Time Correction Logic
APPROVED — A CNA cannot silently overwrite a punch.
APPROVED — A missed or incorrect punch creates a correction request.
APPROVED — Authorized review records the original value, corrected value, user, time, and reason.
APPROVED — Locked timesheets require elevated permission to reopen.
OPEN DECISION — Which admin roles may reopen a locked timesheet.
9. Timesheet Logic
APPROVED — A timesheet is generated from the confirmed assignment and time records.
APPROVED — The facility may review hours or submit a documented discrepancy.
APPROVED — Kivara gives final approval.
APPROVED — Once approved and locked, the same hours feed payroll-ready data and facility billing.
APPROVED — Changes after locking require an audited correction process.
OPEN DECISION — Facility review deadline and what happens if the facility does not respond.
OPEN DECISION — Kivara internal timesheet approval deadline.
10. Payroll-Ready Logic
APPROVED — Only approved and locked hours become payroll ready.
APPROVED — The Kivara app does not calculate or file payroll taxes itself.
APPROVED — The payroll provider handles withholding, tax filings, direct deposit, official pay stubs, and W-2s.
APPROVED — Kivara tracks export/send status and reconciliation status.
OPEN DECISION — Payroll provider and pay schedule.
11. Facility Billing Logic
APPROVED — Approved locked billable hours create invoice data.
APPROVED — Invoice drafts remain reviewable by authorized Kivara users before issue.
APPROVED — Facilities see only their own invoices and billing records.
OPEN DECISION — Bill rates, overtime billing rules, minimum shift charges, cancellation charges, payment terms, and late-payment rules.
12. Credential Logic
APPROVED — Credential status flow: Missing → Uploaded → Pending Review → Approved, Rejected, Expiring Soon, or Expired.
APPROVED — Replacing a credential preserves prior history.
APPROVED — Expiration can automatically affect eligibility according to the credential requirement.
APPROVED — Only authorized Kivara users approve or reject credentials.
OPEN DECISION — Reminder schedule, such as 60/30/14/7 days before expiration.
13. Facility Data Isolation
APPROVED — Each facility company has a private workspace.
APPROVED — Facility users see only authorized company locations.
APPROVED — A multi-location client may have regional users with access to selected locations.
APPROVED — No facility can see another facility's shifts, CNAs, invoices, messages, or documents.
14. Notification Logic
APPROVED — Urgent operational events may use app notification plus text.
APPROVED — Routine events should primarily use in-app notifications and email according to preference.
APPROVED — Shift confirmation notifies both CNA and facility.
APPROVED — Call-outs, no-shows, and at-risk shifts create urgent admin alerts.
OPEN DECISION — Exact notification timing and which alerts users may disable.
15. Audit Trail Logic
APPROVED — Sensitive actions record who acted, what changed, when, previous state, new state, and reason when required.
APPROVED — Audit history applies to confirmed assignments, credentials, GPS/time records, timesheets, pay/bill rates, invoices, and permissions.
APPROVED — The system must not silently delete operational history.
16. Security Logic
APPROVED — Every person has an individual account; shared logins are not allowed.
APPROVED — Privileged Kivara accounts require multi-factor authentication.
APPROVED — Permissions are role based.
APPROVED — Banking details should remain with the payroll provider whenever possible.
APPROVED — Background and drug-screen details are restricted; most workflows use minimum necessary status information.
17. Version 1 Exception Principle
APPROVED — Outside-service outages or GPS failures must not stop the agency from operating.
APPROVED — Authorized manual fallback is allowed only with a reason and audit history.
APPROVED — Exceptions should be visible for review and should not silently bypass business rules.
18. Status Dictionary
19. Open Decision Register
The items marked OPEN DECISION must be decided before the related feature is coded. They are intentionally not guessed in this specification.
20. Developer Acceptance Rule
A feature is not complete merely because a screen exists. The developer must demonstrate that the approved business rule is enforced across CNA, Facility, and Admin experiences, including permissions, status changes, notifications, exceptions, and audit history.
